Last Mile

You're the last delivery driver. Automate every route, swallow every rival, and count the world's independent couriers down to zero.

Last Mile is an incremental / idle delivery-dispatch clicker. You start behind the wheel of a single van, driving stop to stop by hand. By the end you're not driving at all — you're the algorithm the whole city runs on, watching a fleet of pixel-vans swarm a map that keeps zooming out.

From one van to an empire

Drive the first routes yourself — arrows to move, space to deliver, return to the depot to bank the day. Then buy your way out of the driver's seat: Auto-Deliver collects stops you roll over, Autopilot drives for you, and Fleet Recruitment puts a hired van on every column of the map. Layer on Faster Vans, Completion Bonus, passive Contracts, and an Ops Manager that spends your cash so you don't have to.

Zoom out

Every Map Expansion opens new territory and shrinks the grid to fit — chunky early streets become a dense tangle of tiny roads, like pulling back on a map app until the city is just moving dots. Each district generates in its own shape: organic tangles, rigid grids, wide-open blocks, even a maze.

The competition

Expanding isn't free real estate — most new territory belongs to rival courier companies, each a distinct color clustered into its own neighborhoods, running their own vans. The day won't end until their deliveries are done, so every expansion is a bet. Deal with them two ways: Buy Out Rivals to claim a whole company's streets, or unlock Poach Rivals and let your vans steal the stops they drive over — each one pays you and drives down the cost of buying that company out.

Everything counts down

The theme runs through the whole game. Each day's deliveries tick down to zero. Your fleet counts down the rivals. And the number that matters — the share of the map you don't own — falls from 100% toward a single, quiet 0.00%.

Features

  • Hand-driven start that automates into a hands-off idle machine
  • An ever-expanding map that zooms out from streets to specks
  • Rival companies with their own colors, neighborhoods, and delivery vans
  • Buy out rivals wholesale — or poach their stops one by one for a discount
  • Deep upgrade tree: automation, economy, and territory
  • Pick your brand color; progress saves automatically
  • No generative-AI assets — everything is code-drawn

Tagline: Automate the last mile, take over the map, count the competition down to zero.
