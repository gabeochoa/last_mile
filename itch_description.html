Last Mile

Last Mile is an incremental idle delivery game. You just drive around delivering packages until you make enough to start your own fleet of vans. Then ... more stuff happens. Take over rival neighborhoods until you reach 100% market share.

code available @ https://github.com/gabeochoa/last_mile

Controls

  • arrow keys to move
  • space to deliver package
  • mouse for everything else
